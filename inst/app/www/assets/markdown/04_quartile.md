## What is the proportion of men and women in each pay quartile?

### Proportion of men and women in each pay quartile

The chart shows the proportion of men and women in each pay quartile compared to the overall NHSBSA workforce. The proportion of men and women in each quartile, from the lowest to the highest paid, is calculated by dividing the workforce into four equal parts.

This shows that the gender split of the lower to middle pay quartiles favour women. They are over-represented relative to the NHSBSA workforce overall which is 61%, and this influences the gender pay gap. In the highest pay quartile, men and women remain nearly equally represented, with women making up 49% as of March 2025. This marks a slight decrease of 0.8 percentage points from the previous year, suggesting that while overall representation is close to parity, the broader imbalance across pay bands persists.
