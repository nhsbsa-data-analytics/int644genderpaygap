## Actions to remove the gender pay gap

Our <a href="https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/sites/default/files/2025-11/Diversity Inclusion and Social Mobility Strategy 2025-29 %28V1%29 10.2025.pdf" target="_blank">NHSBSA Diversity, Inclusion and Social Mobility Strategy 2025-29</a> outlines our commitment to creating an inclusive organisation with key objectives focussing on being representative of the communities in which we live, work and serve, attracting and retaining diverse talent, building an inclusive and empowering workplace culture, and developing inclusive leadership. In addition, we have a clear focus on supporting those who experience menopause symptoms through our wellbeing work. 
To align to our strategic approach, we have chosen a set of actions that will ensure we continue to review our recruitment methods for inclusivity and unconscious bias, we implement fair and transparent processes for development and pay, we encourage colleagues to access development opportunities that are right for them, we consider the needs of our people in our policies, enabling us to breakdown workplace barriers and create a more inclusive culture and improve leadership understanding and awareness through effective training to embed workplace support. 
Over time, these actions will support greater gender equality across all levels of the organisation and contribute to reducing our gender pay gap.


| Action | Title | Status | Supporting Narrative |
|:---:|:------|:------:|:---------------------|
| 1 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/use-fair-and-structured-interview-techniques" target="_blank">Use fair and structured interview techniques</a> | Embedded | Recruitment and selection training is available to all recruiting managers and colleagues who regularly support hiring. This training covers our strategic aims related to diverse recruitment and talent attraction, addresses potential bias within recruitment processes, and highlights the benefits of building a diverse workforce.<br>We have a clear and consistent scoring process in place to ensure fairness and we communicate the interview structure in advance, providing candidates with clarity and transparency, helping them to prepare effectively and ensuring a more inclusive and equitable recruitment experience for everyone involved. |
| 2 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/make-job-descriptions-inclusive" target="_blank">Make job descriptions inclusive</a> | Embedded | We continue to encourage all hiring managers to use a gender decoding tool in all job adverts to remove subtle gender-based bias. |
| 3 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/use-fair-and-structured-interview-techniques" target="_blank">Use fair and structured interview techniques</a> | Embedded | We continue to utilise diverse panels for our recruitment process, this includes throughout the shortlisting and interviewing process. Having a diverse panel allows us to increase diversity in our hiring process and supports our commitment to improving the recruitment, selection and career progression of women colleagues. |
| 4 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/advertise-flexible-working-arrangements-in-job-adverts" target="_blank">Advertise flexible working arrangement in job adverts</a> | Embedded | We continue to use inclusive job adverts that clearly communicate our commitment to flexible working practices. Each advert highlights our approach, stating that we are committed to being a flexible employer and will aim to offer a working pattern that suits individual needs wherever possible. This includes options such as hybrid working, flexible hours, and other adaptable arrangements. By embedding this message consistently, we aim to attract a diverse talent pool, support different working preferences, and ensure candidates understand our inclusive culture from the outset right from the very start. |
| 5 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/encourage-employee-development-through-actionable-steps" target="_blank">Encourage employee development through actionable steps</a> | Embedded | GROW is designed to support the wellbeing and development of high performing teams through identifying personal objectives. It encourages regular conversations between colleagues on a 1-1 basis, giving the opportunity to have regular check ins with a line manager and have open and honest discussions where development actions are agreed together. |
| 6 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/offer-mentoring-sponsorship-and-other-development-programmes" target="_blank">Offer mentoring, sponsorship and other development programmes</a> | Embedded | Our fourth cohort of Reciprocal Mentoring for Inclusion Programme has recently concluded, this programme is a bespoke programme which pairs colleagues with specific lived experience with a senior leader to learn from each other and share knowledge. These lived experiences include disability and neurodiversity and carers, ethnicity, LGBTQ+, mental health, women and lower socioeconomic background. We are launching our fifth cohort of our Reciprocal Mentoring Programme with a focus on partnerships for women. |
| 7 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/offer-mentoring-sponsorship-and-other-development-programmes" target="_blank">Offer mentoring, sponsorship and other development programmes</a> | New | We have identified internally that while the demographics of our organisation show 61% of our colleagues are female, they tend to saturate lower paid roles with the mean gender pay gap remaining low between bands 2-8a, but between bands 8b-9 the mean gender pay gap begins to rise.<br>We have a newly designed development programme specifically for women in the organisation. The aim of this is to grow and sustain representation at all levels, understand women’s development needs, overcome barriers and embed a women’s centred lens. |
| 8 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/enhance-and-promote-flexible-working-and-leave-policies" target="_blank">Enhance and promote flexible working and leave policies</a> | Embedded | Multiple policies are in place to support women in the workplace, including flexible working arrangements, hybrid working options, special leave provisions, parental leave, carers leave, enhanced maternity leave, and comprehensive sickness absence policies. Together, these initiatives are designed to promote inclusion, improve wellbeing, and enable women to balance professional and personal responsibilities more effectively. They also aim to create an equitable working environment where women feel supported, valued, and able to thrive at all stages of their careers. |
| 9 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/increase-transparency-for-pay-promotion-and-rewards/increase-transparency-for-pay-promotion-and-rewards" target="_blank">Increase transparency for pay, promotion and rewards</a> | Embedded | The NHSBSA follows the Agenda for Change pay rates – a national scheme in place for many NHS colleagues in bands 2-9. This framework gives a transparent outline of the pay rates provided across the NHSBSA and allows for recognition of the time a colleague spends in their role with pay steps provided after 2 years and 5 years respectively. Aligning to a clear pay structure ensures colleagues understand their banding, can easily identify their next pay step and development opportunities. |
| 10 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/conduct-a-menopause-risk-assessment-for-your-workplace" target="_blank">Conduct menopause risk assessments in the workplace</a> | New | We have chosen to introduce menopause risk assessments to better identify workplace factors that may exacerbate menopause symptoms, such as temperature, workload, working patterns and access to facilities. This has been identified from colleague experiences in colleague feedback data. This proactive approach will enable tailored adjustments and reduce barriers to performance and attendance.<br>Progress will be tracked through completion rates of risk assessments, feedback from employees, and monitoring related absence, retention and engagement data. We will also review outcomes regularly to ensure adjustments are effective and refine our approach based on employee experience and organisational need. |
| 11 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/train-managers-to-support-employees-experiencing-menopause" target="_blank">Train managers to support employees experiencing menopause</a> | In progress | We have chosen to embed manager’s training into our managers essentials training programme to ensure they have the knowledge and confidence to hold supportive conversations and respond appropriately to menopause-related needs. This decision is based on colleague experience from colleague feedback data. Manager capability is critical to creating a psychologically safe and inclusive working environment.<br>Progress will be tracked through training completion rates, manager confidence surveys and employee feedback on management support. We will also monitor trends in absence and progression to assess impact, alongside ongoing evaluation to continuously improve the training offer. |
| 12 | <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/offer-workplace-adjustments-for-employees-experiencing-menopause" target="_blank">Offer workplace adjustments for employees experiencing menopause</a> | In progress | We have chosen to strengthen our approach to workplace adjustments to ensure employees experiencing menopause can access a wider offer of timely and appropriate support. This decision is based on colleague experience from colleague feedback data. Adjustments such as flexible working, changes to duties or improved workplace conditions can significantly reduce the impact of symptoms.<br>Progress will be tracked by monitoring the uptake and timeliness of adjustments, employee feedback, and data on absence. Regular reviews will ensure adjustments remain effective and consistent, supporting employees to stay in work and continue to progress. |
