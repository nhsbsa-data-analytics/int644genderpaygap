## Summary

In 2024-25, the mean gender pay gap for hourly pay within the NHSBSA was 12.1%, and the median gap was 8.9%, which is consistent with the previous year. The NHSBSA workforce comprises 61% women and 39% men. The majority (70%) of our colleagues are within the lower pay bands and as these bands have a significantly higher concentration of women, this influences our gender pay gap. The percentage of women in the highest pay quartile has slightly decreased to 49% in 2024-25, following a rise the previous year. While there had been improvement since the dip in 2021, this latest figure suggests that the balance in the higher pay bands still does not offset the concentration of women in the lower quartiles.
