## Summary

In 2025-26, the mean gender pay gap for hourly pay within the NHSBSA was 12%, and the median gap was 11.5%, which has widened slightly from the previous year as more employees joined in the lower middle pay band. The NHSBSA workforce comprises 61% women and 39% men. The majority (69%) of our colleagues are within the lower pay bands and as these bands have a significantly higher concentration of women, this influences our gender pay gap. The percentage of women in the highest pay quartile remained virtually unchanged at 49% in 2025-26. While representation in the highest pay quartile remains close to parity, this still does not offset the concentration of women in the lower pay bands.
