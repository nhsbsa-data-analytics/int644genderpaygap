### Mean and median gender pay gap by AfC band and directorate

The chart illustrates the gender pay gap across various AfC bands and directorates, with an option to view either mean or median hourly pay. In bands 2 to 4, the pay gap is negligible regardless of the metric. Bands 8C, 8D and 9 all show a pay gap favouring men. Band 9 improved notably, falling from 10% to 2% on the mean and 13% to 5% on the median, though findings should be interpreted with caution given the small number of colleagues in this band. Band 8D shifted direction, moving from marginally favouring women in 2025 to a mean gap of 5% and median gap of 6% favouring men in 2026. Band 8C followed a similar pattern, with the gap widening from around 2% to between 4% and 5% on both measures.

In non-AfC pay bands, women represent 61% of the workforce. Yet, they are more commonly found in roles associated with lower pay when grouped by hourly earnings.
